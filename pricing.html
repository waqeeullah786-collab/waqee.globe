Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about SWAZ pricing.

Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. Cancel any time from your account settings — no questions asked, no cancellation fees.
Is there a free trial on paid plans? +
Our Free plan gives you 7 searches per day forever. Pro and Business plans include a 7-day free trial — no card required to start.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit and debit cards via Stripe. Annual business plans can be paid by bank transfer — contact us for details.
Can I switch plans later? +
Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Prorated credits are applied automatically to your next bill.
Do you offer refunds? +
Yes — we offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied, email us and we'll refund you in full.
