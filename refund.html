Refund Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

1. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a full 7-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you are not satisfied for any reason within 7 days of your first payment, email us and we will issue a full refund — no questions asked.

2. Eligibility

Refund requests must be made within 7 days of the original purchase date. Requests after this period will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis at our discretion.

3. How to Request a Refund

Email support@swaz.app with your account email and the reason for your request. We aim to process all refunds within 3–5 business days.

4. Non-Refundable Items

Add-on credits that have already been consumed and one-time data exports are non-refundable once used.

5. Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. We do not offer prorated refunds for unused subscription time beyond the 7-day window.

6. Contact

For refund-related questions, email support@swaz.app.
