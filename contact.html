Common questions

How quickly can you start on a project? +
We typically start within 1–3 business days of signing off on scope and receiving an initial payment. Rush timelines are available for an additional fee.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance? +
Yes. All projects include 30 days of free support post-delivery. After that, we offer monthly retainer plans for ongoing maintenance, updates, and new features.
What's your payment structure? +
We work on a 50/50 model — 50% upfront to begin, 50% on delivery. For larger projects we can arrange milestone-based payments.
Can you work with my existing tech stack? +
Absolutely. We adapt to your stack. Whether you're on AWS, GCP, Vercel, Netlify, or a self-hosted server — we'll build and deploy in your environment.
