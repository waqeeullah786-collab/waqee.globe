🏢 About Us

Built by sellers,
for sellers

SWAZ was born out of frustration with guesswork. We built the tool we always wished existed.

Our story

SWAZ started as an internal tool at Waqee Global Tech, a Pakistan-based e-commerce consultancy. After years of helping clients find profitable products manually — spending hours on spreadsheets — we decided to automate the process.

Today, SWAZ serves hundreds of Amazon and Walmart sellers worldwide. Our mission is simple: make data-driven product research accessible to every seller, not just the big players with big budgets.

We are a small, product-focused team. Every feature we ship is tested by real sellers before it goes live. We eat our own cooking.
