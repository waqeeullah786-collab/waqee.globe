Our Story

From a single workflow
to a full product company.

waqeeglobe.info started with a simple frustration: why do sellers still spend hours doing research that software could do in seconds? We set out to fix that — first for ourselves, then for thousands of sellers worldwide.

We're a software and SaaS company specializing in AI-powered automation. Our stack is centered around n8n, custom AI agents, and full-stack SaaS development — with a focus on products that actually move the needle.

Today, our products are used by 20,000+ sellers across Amazon and Walmart, and we continue to build tools that save hours, reduce errors, and unlock new revenue.

We're not just developers — we're product people who obsess over the user experience, the data pipeline, and the business outcome that our software delivers.
